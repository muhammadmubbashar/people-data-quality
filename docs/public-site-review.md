# Public-site review and project direction

Reviewed 29 October 2026. This is a limited public-content review, not an audit of private systems.

The consumer homepage presents flights, holidays, stays, tours, cruises, deals, quotes, stores,
booking support and World360 Rewards. Visible links include Cloudinary-hosted media and a
Google Tag Manager iframe. Those observations do not identify the HR platform's architecture.

The analyst advert explicitly names a People Systems auditing tool, Workday, local payroll and
Core, alongside investigations, reporting, testing, coaching and requirements gathering.
A related GPS APAC vacancy describes HR, payroll, Finance, IT and Workday functional colleagues.
Its application portal is powered by PageUp. A recruitment portal is not evidence of payroll design.

Unknowns: the audit product, Core's role and APIs, payroll vendors, hosting, identity permissions,
integration schedules, field ownership and actual correction workflows.

Recommendation: build an independent HR operations practice lab. Recreating the public marketing
website would provide weak evidence for this particular job. Use synthetic employees and clearly
label fictional policies. Native Workday operation still requires authorised tenant access.
