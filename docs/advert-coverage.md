# Advert coverage: practice evidence and boundaries

All eleven advertised duty areas have a practice representation. This is not evidence of performing
them inside Flight Centre, and no private implementation has been inferred from the public website.

1. **Support audit users:** audit queue, source comparison, evidence and next-action notes.
2. **Coach HR, Payroll and business users:** coaching prompts, teach-back records and guides.
   Self-practice is labelled separately; real coaching requires an actual learner.
3. **Investigate exceptions and root causes:** ownership, priority, escalation, uncertainty and
   recorded root cause. A selected cause is an analyst assertion, not automatic proof.
4. **Guide Workday corrections:** request/review/apply simulation with role checks and revalidation.
   Real Workday screens, business processes and permissions still require authorised tenant access.
5. **Support bulk corrections, testing and approvals:** batch preview, review, all-or-nothing
   execution, stale-value rejection and repeated-application protection.
6. **Align Workday, payroll and Core data:** synthetic source comparison, date checks and targeted
   corrections. Actual Core meaning, mappings, vendors and integration ownership are unverified.
7. **Build basic Workday reports:** reporting filters and a report planning exercise. Native Workday
   report construction has not been implemented or demonstrated without a tenant.
8. **Identify recurring trends:** comparable dated captures, counts and recorded causes. No claims
   of real savings, company defect rates or predictive accuracy.
9. **Validation/regression tests and defects:** executable fictional releases, intentional failure,
   defect evidence and reviewer-verified passing retest. Not actual Workday release testing.
10. **Maintain knowledge resources:** searchable guides, FAQs and retained document versions.
11. **Stakeholder relationships and requirements:** simulated requirements, acceptance criteria and
    decisions. Relationships, workshops and collaboration must be demonstrated with real people.

## Candidate abilities this can support

Data comparison, attention to detail, exception handling, documented reasoning, process discipline,
learning a complex workflow and explaining findings can be demonstrated by completing the exercises
yourself. CSV downloads provide Excel practice; proficiency needs your own demonstration. This lab
does not confer Workday certification, HR employment history, payroll expertise or production readiness.
